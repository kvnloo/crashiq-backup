Tennessee: ready, 2021 to 2024 complete

TDOT publishes every Tennessee crash from 2021 to 2024, with its spot on the map and the full injury scale (fatal, serious, minor, possible, property damage). No login. CrashIQ now reads it. For 2025 on, the Department of Safety's map has the fatal and injury crashes, and Nashville's open data adds the fender-benders there.

What we found

  • About 190,000 crashes a year, 2021-2024, every one with a point.
  • Real injury levels. Example, Nashville (Davidson County) 2024: 106 fatal, 484 serious, 5317 minor, 2247 possible-injury and 17,536 property-damage crashes.
  • No street names. TDOT gives a route number (SR 11, I-24) at most. Crashes are placed by their point.
  • 2025 on is thinner: the state's public map has only injury crashes. Inside Nashville, the city's data adds 18,488 no-injury crashes for 2025.

Test run

Nolensville Pike, Nashville, 0.92 mi, 2021-2024: 406 crashes counted (3 fatal, 9 serious, 107 minor, 41 possible, 246 property damage). PDF and Excel built.

What we didn't check

  • How close TDOT's map points are to the real crash spot (they are snapped to TDOT's roads).
  • Memphis's own crash layers.
  • Tennessee crash costs (TDOT publishes no table) and traffic counts for rates.
