Vermont: crash data for CrashIQ

Hermes, 2026-11-08. Status: active.

Where it comes from

  • VTrans' public crash map layer, free, no login. Every crash report since 2010, updated almost daily.
  • About 7,000-7,600 reportable crashes a year. 99% have a map point.

What's missing

  • No injury levels: a crash is fatal, injury or property damage only. Example: on Shelburne Rd the report shows 54 injury crashes as 'level not reported', none as A, B or C.
  • No people counts and no driver or vehicle records, so no cost column and no road-user movements.

Can we use it in a paid product?

  • Unclear. VTrans gives a disclaimer, not a licence: "Users assume responsibility for the queries they create."

Placing crashes with no point

  • On 2,000 Chittenden crashes with the point hidden, CrashIQ placed 31% automatically from street names; 79% landed within 250 ft.
  • That's weaker than other states: officers often write a business or parking lot as the cross street ('Goss Dodge Chrysler'). It matters only for the 1-2% of crashes with no point.

A real test

  • Shelburne Rd (US 7), South Burlington, 1 mile, 2020-2024: 449 crashes counted, 4 fatal (two pedestrians, one cyclist, all near Fayette Rd), 54 injury, 391 property damage.

What you need to do

  • Ask VTrans for written OK to show this data in CrashIQ.
  • Optional: ask VTrans for current statewide crash rates.

What we didn't check

  • House-address placement (the national address service was down).
  • Whether VTrans' point or our placement is wrong on the far misses.
