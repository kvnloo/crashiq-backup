Alaska: every crash, 2021–2025

In one line: last week's screen said Alaska was request-only. It isn't: DOT&PF's Public Crash Portal serves every crash of the last five years with a map point and a K A B C O level, no login. No street names, no people counts, and no licence that says a paid tool may use it.

1. What Alaska has

Crashes7,900–9,300 a year (2024: 9,209). Anchorage is 58%.

3. A real corridor

East Tudor Road, Lake Otis Parkway to Elmore Road, Anchorage, 1.04 mi, 2021–2025: 349 crashes: 1 fatal, 6 serious, 58 minor, 48 possible, 236 property damage. Crash cost $60.5 million at DOT&PF's prices. PDF and Excel exports work.

What you need to do

  1. Ask DOT&PF's crash data team (dot.crash.info@alaska.gov) in writing if a paid tool may read the Public Crash Portal. The portal hides its download button; CrashIQ reads the same feed its map uses, which they could change. One line flips the pack to "not offered" if you'd rather wait.
  2. Optional: ask the same team for the full export (it has street names and injury counts) if you want those in Alaska reports.

What we didn't check

The licence; how long the feed stays open; Anchorage city data (only high-crash maps, no crash list); the alcohol and speeding flags.
