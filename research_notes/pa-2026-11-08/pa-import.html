Pennsylvania: every reportable crash, 2006–2025

In one line: PennDOT publishes every reportable crash, one zip per county and year, no login: a map point on 99.5% of them, street names on 99.9%, and the crash's worst injury (K A B C O). It hides the day of the month, and there is no licence that says a paid tool may use it.

1. What Pennsylvania has

WhatExample
Map point99.5–99.9% each year (2025: 109,698 crashes).
Street namesEvery road in the crash, in order: BROAD ST then GIRARD AV.
Injury levelMAX_SEVERITY_LEVEL 1–4 = K A B C. About 11% say "injury, unknown severity" or "unknown if injured": counted as injury, level not reported (U), as PennDOT's own Crash Facts does.
MissingThe day of the month (month, weekday and hour only). People and vehicle tables exist but CrashIQ doesn't read them yet.

2. Does it place without GPS? Yes

99.1%
of Philadelphia crashes CrashIQ placed from the two street names alone (GPS hidden) landed within 250 ft of PennDOT's point (Pittsburgh's county: 97.3%). Example: SIXTYTHIRD ST is now read as 63rd St, which took Philadelphia from 68% to 93% placed.

Since PennDOT already has a point for 97.6–99.5% of crashes, this adds only the few without one (38 of 7,014 in Philadelphia 2025).

3. A real corridor

North Broad Street, Girard to Susquehanna, Philadelphia, 1.03 mi, 2021–2025: 375 crashes: 6 fatal, 29 serious, 103 minor, 50 possible, 102 injury-level-unknown, 85 property damage. Crash cost $155.3 million (PennDOT's 2025 costs). PDF and Excel exports work.
