In one line: Open yearly Excel workbooks, record-level, statewide, 100% GPS, KABCO per person. Catches: whole-year downloads (~100 MB), no manner of collision, no reuse licence.

1. Where it comes from

eCrash public data (Mississippi DPS), open (yearly .xlsx, no login).
https://publicdata.dps.ms.gov/Public-CrashData/eCrash/

2. Can we sell it?

unclear. No licence. 'These data sets are typically used to respond to public records requests and made available to the public free of charge.'

3. Does it place?

100% of 2,000 crashes in Hinds County (28049), 2024 have a map point. Hiding the point and placing by the two street names lands 74% within 250 ft (at least 69%).
