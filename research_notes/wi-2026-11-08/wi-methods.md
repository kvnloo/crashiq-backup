# Wisconsin methods (cited)

Manual: WisDOT Facilities Development Manual 11-38, Safety Certification Process, August 15, 2025
(https://wisconsindot.gov/rdwy/fdm/fd-11-38.pdf, sha256 fb4d8d99…a709, in `~/.hermes/state-manuals/wi/`).

1. **Crash costs**, p. 15, §10.5.3.2, Table 10.2 "Crash Costs for Benefit-Cost Analysis in 2025 Dollars", per crash by
   most severe injury: K $16,085,401; A $818,160; B $258,751; C $146,882; O $19,499. Built with FHWA's Crash Costs for
   Highway Safety Analysis method.
2. **Years of data**, p. 13, Step 4.1a: five years by default; at least two after a geometric or control change; up to
   ten for stable rural sites (with BTO's agreement).
3. **Statewide rates / screening**: FDM 11-38 sends these to TEOpS 12-3 (not read): missing. Screening uses the HSM
   critical rate against the corridor's own sites (national, user's decision).

Differences from California: WisDOT's own 2025 costs; HSM predictive method (IHSDM) is WisDOT's practice, not built.
