Wisconsin: crash data for CrashIQ

Hermes, 2026-11-08. Status: active, but only through 2022.

Where it comes from

  • WisDOT's yearly crash files, free download, no login. 2017 to 2022.
  • About 129,000 crashes in 2022; 99% have a map point, and every one names its street and cross street.
  • WisDOT hasn't posted 2023 or later. Studies default to 2018-2022.

Severity

  • Real KABCO on every crash. Example: Capitol Drive had 3 fatal and 17 serious-injury crashes in 5 years.

Can we use it in a paid product?

  • Unclear. WisDOT calls it "open data" but gives no licence.
  • The newer data in Community Maps is off limits: its terms forbid automated or bulk downloads.

Costs

  • WisDOT's own 2025 values per crash: fatal $16.1M, serious $818,160, minor $258,751, possible $146,882, property damage $19,499.

Street names

  • With the point hidden, CrashIQ placed 75% of 2,000 Milwaukee crashes from street names; 97% landed within 250 ft. On Capitol Drive it also pulled in 14 crashes that had no point at all.

A real test

  • W Capitol Drive, 27th to 43rd St, Milwaukee (1 mi), 2018-2022: 1015 crashes: 3 fatal, 17 serious, 114 minor, 175 possible, 706 property damage. Cost $131,133,181. 11 findings.

What you need to do

  • Nothing to start.
  • Optional: email DSPTrafficCrashes@dot.wi.gov to ask when 2023+ will be posted, and if paid use is OK.

What we didn't check

  • Placing crashes by house number.
  • WisDOT's statewide average crash rates (in a manual we didn't read).
  • Driver and passenger records (not read).
