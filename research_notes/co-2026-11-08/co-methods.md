# Colorado methods (Hermes, 2026-11-08)

Manuals: none saved yet. CDOT's crash costs are published in its HSIP annual report, hosted only on FHWA's site,
which answers 403 to scripted downloads (curl, headless Chrome). The values below were read through a text
extractor of the live PDF on 2026-11-08; they are `verified: false` in `data/reference/methods_co.json` until a copy
is saved and hashed, so CrashIQ shows no Colorado crash costs yet.

| Value | Colorado | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Crash costs | K $1,952,000 · A $1,112,000 · B $242,000 · C $132,000 **per person**; O $18,000 per crash (2025 $) | CDOT, *Colorado HSIP 2025 Annual Report* (to FHWA), Program Methodology, "CDOT 2025 Crash Costs", p. 11 of 42 |
| How costs apply | CDOT's B/C uses these; CrashIQ prices each crash once at its own level, which undercounts crashes with several people hurt. The listing has people per level (Injury 01-04), so per-person pricing is possible later | same |
| Statewide average rates by facility type | missing: CDOT screens with its own SPFs (Level of Service of Safety) by facility and ADT, not average rates | CDOT "Safety Analysis & Information" page; HSIP 2025 report pp. 9-10 |
| Screening | CDOT: LOSS (SPF + EB) and crash-pattern diagnostics, then B/C. CrashIQ uses the HSM critical rate against the corridor's own sites | HSIP 2025 report pp. 9-10 of 42; HSM Ch. 4 |
| Minimum years | missing (CDOT's crash-data page asks requesters for "up to five years") | codot.gov crash-data page |
| SPF calibration | missing | — |
