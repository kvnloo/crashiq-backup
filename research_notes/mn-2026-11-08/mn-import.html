Minnesota: crash data for CrashIQ

Hermes, 2026-11-08. Status: active.

Where it comes from

  • Minnesota Public Safety's MNCrash website, free, no login. 2016 to now.
  • About 64,000 crashes in 2024; all but 4 have a map point.
  • There's no data feed: CrashIQ fills in the website's search form the way you would and downloads the result.

Severity

  • Real KABCO for each crash. 3% have none; CrashIQ uses the death and injury counts. Example: Lake St had 54 such crashes; none of them counted an injury.

Can we use it in a paid product?

  • Unclear. The site has no licence or terms. It's public government data, but nothing grants paid use.

Costs

  • MnDOT's own 2026 values per crash: fatal $16.5M, serious $1.7M, minor $380,000, possible $180,000, property damage $18,000.

What's missing

  • No driver, passenger or pedestrian records. So CrashIQ can't pick out pedestrian or bike crashes in Minnesota.
  • No crash type (rear end, angle…), lighting or weather.

Street names

  • With the point hidden, CrashIQ placed 31% of 2,000 Hennepin crashes from street names; 71% landed within 250 ft. Half the reports name just one street. Not needed, since nearly every crash has a point.

A real test

  • Lake Street, Lyndale to Portland, Minneapolis (0.9 mi), 2020-2024: 519 crashes: 3 fatal, 17 serious, 65 minor, 143 possible, 291 property damage. Cost $134,078,000.

What you need to do

  • Nothing to start.
  • Optional: email MNCrash.Team@state.mn.us to ask if paid use is OK, and for their full files with person records.

What we didn't check

  • Row counts for years other than 2024.
  • Why Lake St excluded 541 of the 1060 crashes pulled (likely cross-street crashes; not broken down).
  • Freeways other than Interstates count as arterials.
