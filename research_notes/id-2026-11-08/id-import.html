Idaho: crash data for CrashIQ

Hermes, 8 Nov 2026. Status: ready to use (active), with two catches.

What Idaho has

  • ITD (the state DOT) publishes every police-reported crash on a public map service, no login. About 22,000 to 28,000 crashes a year.
  • Every crash since 2008 has a GPS point. Example: crash 22C608312 in Boise sits at 43.5698, -116.2167.
  • Severity is the standard K/A/B/C/O scale. Each vehicle and person is linked to its crash, so we can flag pedestrian, bicycle, motorcycle and truck crashes.

Catch 1: it's about three years behind

The newest full year is 2022. 2023 stops on 1 Oct 2023 (18,488 of the 27,679 crashes ITD's own report counts), and 2024 and 2025 aren't there. CrashIQ treats a part-loaded year as missing and never counts it. The default study window is 2018-2022.

Catch 2: no street names

Crashes are placed by GPS only. A crash in a parking lot next to the corridor can't be told apart from one on the corridor, so check those by hand.

Test run: Fairview Ave, Boise (Orchard to Cole, 1.55 mi, 2018-2022)

  • 230 crashes counted: K 0, A 8, B 24, C 40, no-injury 158.
  • Crash cost $14,406,270 at ITD's 2025 costs (fatal $14.2M, serious injury $679,115).
  • PDF and Excel exports both work (33 KB and 33 KB). No over-representation findings came up on this corridor.

Licence

ITD gives the data "as is" and says it's "the sole responsibility of the user to determine the usability". It neither grants nor forbids paid use, so the licence is unclear.

What you need to do

  1. Email ITD's Office of Highway Safety (ohsweb@itd.idaho.gov) for written permission to use the crash layers in CrashIQ.
  2. In the same email, ask them to load 2023-2025 into the public service (their dashboard already shows those years).

What we didn't check

  • How accurate ITD's GPS points are on local streets.
  • Statewide average crash rates (ITD doesn't publish them by road type) and Idaho traffic counts.
  • The COMPASS (Boise-area) copy of the data.
