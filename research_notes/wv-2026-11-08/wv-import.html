West Virginia: email only

WVDOT keeps every police crash report in one database, but the only way in is to email one staff member. Nothing is online. So there's no West Virginia pack yet.

What we found

  • WVDOT's crash page is one line: "To obtain traffic accident data, please contact Marsha Mays" (Marsha.K.Mays@wv.gov).
  • WVDOT's online maps have no crashes. We searched its 326 map services and its GIS server. They hold roads, signs, bridges and traffic counts. The only safety layers are county totals, for example "Ohio County: 4.6 deaths in 2013".
  • A consultant's copy shows what the file will look like. A firm that did WVDOT's pedestrian study posted 297 fatal and serious pedestrian crashes. 97% have a GPS point, and each has a route and milepost, for example "Kanawha WV 61, mile 23.13". It's their copy, not WVDOT's, so we didn't use it as data.

What you need to do

  • Email Marsha.K.Mays@wv.gov for 2021-2025 statewide crashes, with vehicles, people, GPS points, route and milepost, street names, and the field list, as CSV.
  • Ask in the same email whether a paid tool may use the data.
  • Send us the file you get. The column map is ready.

What we didn't check

  • How well crashes place (no file yet).
  • West Virginia's crash costs and rates.
  • City feeds (we found none).
