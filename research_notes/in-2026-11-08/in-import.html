Indiana: locked, needs your account

Indiana keeps its crash reports in ARIES, run by LexisNexis for the State Police. There is no free download of the crashes. You get them through INDOT's AASHTOWare Safety tool (an account INDOT approves), a paid LexisNexis request, or one report at a time on BuyCrash.

What we found

  • No open crash file. The ARIES site posts only yearly statistics (a 2025 PDF). Indiana's data hub has 0 crash datasets.
  • The open maps are partial. Example: Indianapolis' MPO shares only fatal and serious-injury crashes (8,753 since 2018). A state map layer stops in 2016.
  • INDOT's methods are public and ready. Crash costs per crash (2025 $): fatal and serious $857,956, B $212,262, C $125,600, property damage $15,587. Five years of data.

What you need to do

  • Make an Access Indiana account (access.in.gov), log into ITAP (itap.indot.in.gov), choose "Enroll New Application", then AASHTOWare Safety. Or ask an INDOT project manager to request the crash data from INDOT's Office of Traffic Safety.
  • Export 5 years (2021-2025) of crashes for your counties as CSV, with latitude/longitude and road names, and send us the file's first row. We'll build the reader.
  • Ask INDOT in writing if that export may be used in a paid tool.

What we didn't check

  • The real export columns (need the account).
  • City portals in Fort Wayne, Evansville and South Bend.
