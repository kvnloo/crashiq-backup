Kentucky: ready, with one big catch

Kentucky State Police's public crash search gives every crash with its GPS point, free and without a login. CrashIQ now reads it. The catch: it says only killed / injured / nobody hurt, not how badly someone was hurt.

What we found

  • Every crash has a GPS point. In Louisville, Jan-Feb 2025: 1,970 crashes, all with a point.
  • Street names are there too. Half the crashes give a road and a cross street ("PRESTON & ROSEMARY"). Hiding the GPS and placing from the names alone put 44% of crashes on the map, 87% of them within 250 ft of the real spot.
  • No injury levels. A crash is fatal, injury or property damage. So "serious injury (A)" counts and crash costs can't be done. Example: Bardstown Rd shows 58 injury crashes, all as "level not reported".
  • Years: 2015 to today, updated daily.

Test run

Bardstown Rd, Louisville, 0.84 mi, 2020-2024: 294 crashes counted (0 fatal, 58 injury, 236 property damage). PDF and Excel built.

What you need to do

  • Ask Kentucky State Police in writing whether CrashIQ may show this data in a paid tool. The site has no licence, only a "no warranty" note.
  • Optional: to get injury levels, ask KYTC for a data agreement (their CDAT tool has them).

What we didn't check

  • Whether KSP minds automated searches.
  • Kentucky's crash costs (KYTC uses the National Safety Council's, no table of its own).
  • Traffic counts for crash rates.
