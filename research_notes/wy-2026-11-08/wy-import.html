Wyoming: crash data is by request

In one line: WYDOT doesn't post crash records; you ask for them (about 10 business days). Only Cheyenne has an open map layer, with no licence. No Wyoming pack yet.

1. What Wyoming has

WhatExample
StatewideWYDOT crash data request form. Public: county counts, fatal crash app, a yearly report.
WYDOT map serverRoads, mileposts, traffic counts, roadkill. No crashes.
Cheyenne8,440 crashes 2020–2024, every one with a point and K A B C O U. Street names like E 20TH ST & LOGAN AVE.
LicenceNone stated, for WYDOT or for Cheyenne.

2. If you get WYDOT's file

The crash report form records a GPS point, the street and the cross street, and each person's injury level, so a WYDOT export would place like South Dakota's. The notes list the columns to ask for (crash ID, date, time, GPS, street 1, street 2, county, KABCO, killed, injured).

What you need to do

  1. Send WYDOT's crash data request (dot.state.wy.us, Highway Safety → Crash Data; leave the drop-down as it is) for a crash-level export of the counties and years you need, and ask if a paid tool may use it.
  2. If Cheyenne matters to you: ask the Cheyenne and Laramie County GIS Cooperative if their crash layer may be used.
  3. Ask WYDOT for crash costs per injury level.

What we didn't check

A real WYDOT export, street-name placement on Cheyenne's layer, the Casper layer (it's on a private network).
