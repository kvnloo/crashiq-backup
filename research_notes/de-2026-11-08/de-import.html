Delaware: every reported crash on the map, 2009–2026

In one line: Delaware's Department of Safety and Homeland Security publishes every crash since 2009 with a map point, no login. It gives only fatal / injury / property damage (no A B C), no street names and no people, and no licence for a paid tool.

1. What Delaware has

WhatExample
Map point591,221 of 591,222 crashes (2026-11-08).
Injury levelCrash class only: Fatality Crash (K), Personal Injury Crash (counted as injury, level not reported), Property Damage Only. KSI = fatal only.
Crash detailsManner of impact (Angle, Front to rear), light, weather, surface, pedestrian/bike/motorcycle, alcohol, drugs, work zone, contributing circumstance.
Left out"Non-reportable" crashes (about 1 in 4): parking lots and private property, or damage under $2,000. DelDOT's safety program counts fatal, injury and property-damage crashes only.
MissingStreet names, road type, report number, drivers, vehicles, people killed/injured.

3. A real corridor

Kirkwood Highway at Prices Corner, 0.91 mi, 2021–2025: 470 crashes: 0 fatal, 57 injury, 413 property damage. No dollar cost: DelDOT hasn't published crash costs yet. PDF and Excel exports work.

What you need to do

  1. Ask DSHS (or the data.delaware.gov team) in writing if a paid tool may load Public Crash Data, and what notice to show.
  2. Decide: keep leaving out non-reportable crashes (now), or count them as property damage?
  3. Ask DelDOT for the Delaware crash costs it is finishing in 2026.
