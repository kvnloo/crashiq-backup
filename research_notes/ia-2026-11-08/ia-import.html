Iowa: crash data for CrashIQ

Hermes, 2026-11-08. Status: active.

Where it comes from

  • Iowa DOT's public crash layers (the data behind ICAT), free, no login. 2015 to now, updated daily.
  • About 53,000 crashes a year, every one with a map point.

Severity

  • Real KABCO: Iowa counts fatal, major, minor and possible injuries per crash. 'Unknown injury' shows as 'level not reported'. Example: Merle Hay Road has 30 such crashes out of 111 injury crashes.

Can we use it in a paid product?

  • Yes, with credit. CC BY 4.0: "for any purpose, including commercial use, provided that you give appropriate credit". The report's legal line credits Iowa DOT.

Costs

  • Iowa DOT's own safety-program costs (2018 dollars): fatal and major injury $3.25M each, minor $217,607, possible $102,564, property damage $12,656.

Street names

  • With the point hidden, CrashIQ placed 38% of 2,000 Polk crashes from street names; 96% within 250 ft. Not needed for placement (every crash has a point), but it shows the street check works.

A real test

  • Merle Hay Road, 1 mile, 2020-2024: 346 crashes counted: 0 fatal, 3 serious, 23 minor, 55 possible, 30 unknown, 235 property damage. Cost $23,380,188.
