Wild-animal hits are ~24% of SD crashes (4,529 of 18,974 in 2025); SDDOT's RSI screening leaves them out. CrashIQ counts them (they show as `WILD ANIMAL HIT - DAMAGE ONLY` / `ANIMAL - WILD`).
