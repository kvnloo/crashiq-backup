South Dakota: every crash, 2011–2025

In one line: South Dakota posts every crash, one file a year, no login: a map point on all of them, street names on 98%, and each person's injury level (K A B C O). There's no licence that says a paid tool may use it.

1. What South Dakota has

WhatExample
Crashes17,600–19,500 a year (2025: 18,974).
Map pointEvery crash (one 2025 row was garbled and dropped).
Street namesRoad and nearest cross street with distance: N LINCOLN AVE, 101 ft S of W BURNSIDE ST.
Injury levelPer person: fatal, incapacitating, non-incapacitating, possible, none = K A B C O.
MissingCrash costs per level, statewide average rates.

2. Does it place without GPS? Mostly

91%
of Sioux Falls crashes CrashIQ placed from the street names alone (GPS hidden) landed within 250 ft of the state's point. Since every crash already has a point, this is only a cross-check.

3. A real corridor

South Minnesota Avenue, 41st to 57th Street, Sioux Falls, 0.97 mi, 2020–2024: 340 crashes: 0 fatal, 2 serious, 17 minor, 53 possible, 268 property damage. No dollar total (South Dakota publishes no crash costs per level). PDF and Excel exports work.

What you need to do

  1. Ask SD Public Safety (arinfo@state.sd.us) in writing if a paid tool may load its raw crash data, and what notice to show.
  2. Decide on crash costs: SDDOT has none per injury level.

What we didn't check

The 2011–2019 file columns, contributing-circumstance and DUI flags, house addresses.
