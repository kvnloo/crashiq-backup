CrashIQ · state check · 8 Nov 2026 · written by Hermes

Georgia: locked. No state pack yet.

In one line: GDOT keeps every crash in GEARS, and only approved users can get in. Its public dashboard shows charts, with no download. The Atlanta Regional Commission shares 1,074,688 metro crashes openly, but only with the year, not the date.

1. What Georgia has

SourceCan CrashIQ use it?
GEARS (all crash reports)Login only. GDOT's request form says the data "is not to be sold to anyone".
GDOT Crash Data DashboardCharts and a map, last 5 years. No download.
ARC "Crashes 2020-2024" (19 metro counties)Open, with a map point and K A B C O. But only the year (2024, not 6 Mar 2024), no time and no licence.

What you need to do

  1. Ask GDOT (Crash Reporting Unit, GEARS line 866-495-4206) for a statewide crash export, with crashes, vehicles and people, and written permission to use it in a paid tool.

  3. When an export arrives, the column map is ready: ga-column-map.json.
