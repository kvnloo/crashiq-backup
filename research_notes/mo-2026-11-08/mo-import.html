Missouri: ready (last week's screen was wrong)

The Highway Patrol's public crash map sits on an open data layer with every Missouri crash report since 2013: the GPS point, the street and cross street, and how bad it was. No login. CrashIQ now reads it.

What we found

  • About 140,000-155,000 crashes a year (2013-2023). In Kansas City's county, 2023: 19,822 crashes, 95% with GPS.
  • Street names work. The officer writes the street, the cross street and the distance ("NW Gregory Blvd, 205 ft east of NW Lake Dr", Lee's Summit). Hiding the GPS and placing from the names put 62% of crashes on the map automatically, 96% of them within 250 ft of the real spot. By that rate CrashIQ can place about 4 in 10 of the 5% with no GPS (estimate).
  • Injury levels are partial: fatal, serious and "minor" (minor and possible lumped together). So B and C can't be told apart.
  • Fender-benders stop in 2024. 2023 has 101,342 property-damage crashes; 2024 has 24,623; 2025 has none yet.

Test run

Troost Ave, Kansas City, 0.99 mi, 2020-2024: 307 crashes counted (1 fatal, 5 serious, 116 minor, 185 property damage). PDF and Excel built.
