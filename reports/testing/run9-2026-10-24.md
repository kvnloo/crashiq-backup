## 9C on hidden police GPS

streets on the union. The GPS is hidden. The pool is a seeded yardstick sample of 10,467, plus 566 crashes that fit
only with TIGER. Each crash is placed with the app's locator and gate on OSM alone and on the union. Right = within
250 ft.

## Freeway crashes (D1, 24,076 in run 9A)

**Waterfall.** Each crash is counted once, at the first step that would place it:

| Step | Crashes | Fatal | Fatal + serious |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 GPS repaired (#247) | 3,139 | 1 | 88 |
| 2 GPS agrees with the report's spot | 1,925 | 8 | 56 |
| 3 report spot found (#246, by the report) | 8,657 | 13 | 217 |
| 4 GPS on the named freeway, no report spot (review only) | 2,247 | 17 | 67 |
| 5 nothing | 8,108 | 24 | 318 |

Step 4's control: a random 2 km shift still lands on the named freeway 395 times. That's why step 4 is review only.

**Step 3 accuracy.** Truth is a seeded sample of 6,000 freeway crashes whose police GPS fits the report (run 9A
gps_usable). The report's spot is computed with the GPS unused.

| Within | Mainline (n 3,968) | 95% low | Fwy-to-fwy (n 227) | 95% low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 400 ft | 73.5% | 72.1% | 63.9% | 57.4% |
| 1,000 ft | 86.6% | 85.5% | 77.5% | 71.7% |
| 0.25 mi | 89.5% | 88.6% | 80.6% | 75.0% |
| 0.5 mi | 94.6% | 93.9% | 92.1% | 87.8% |
| 1 mi | 97.7% | 97.2% | 97.8% | 94.9% |

**Same Caltrans 0.5-mi segment as the GPS.** Both points are snapped to the Caltrans postmile layer, and a segment is
route, county, prefix and floor(PM / 0.5). Mainline: 82.5% same segment (low 81.3%), 95.4% same or adjacent (low
94.7%). Fwy-to-fwy: 70.7% and 87.6%. Fixed bins cut small errors at their edges, so "same segment" understates
segment-level accuracy.

User decision 2026-10-24 (docs/03, #256): no-GPS freeway crashes are placed by the report and labelled approximate
(±0.5 mi), about a 95% band. Moving a GPS point when the report disagrees with it is not decided; no GPS is moved.

**Why the misses miss.** 1,051 mainline misses (more than 400 ft), one class each:

| Class | Crashes | Share |
|---|---|---|
| vi same side, farther than stated | 331 | 31.5% |
| iv same side, shorter than stated | 272 | 25.9% |
| i opposite side of the crossing | 208 | 19.8% |
| v other route or carriageway | 154 | 14.7% |
| iii stated direction across the freeway | 78 | 7.4% |
| ii another crossing of the same street | 8 | 0.8% |

**Direction of travel, class i.** "Report upstream, GPS downstream" 91 vs "report downstream, GPS upstream" 81. That's
not lopsided, so the GPS isn't systematically where vehicles stopped. The 1,000-hit baseline puts 920 of 973 on the same
side.

**CHP postmiles as a third source.**
- **Fill rate:** CCRS has `MilepostMarker` / `Distance` / `Direction` / `UnitOfMeasure`, which the mirror drops. CHP
  filled them on 3.7% of 2021 and 2.3% of 2022 freeway crashes, and essentially never from 2023. D1 has 830 of 24,076.
- **Placement:** of 14,598 CHP 2021–22 crashes with a marker, 10,697 land on Caltrans SHN Postmiles Tenth (downloaded
  2026-10-24, user-approved).
- **Accuracy:** the postmile alone is within 400 ft of GPS on 40.7% of crashes. With MilepostDistance/Direction
  applied it's 63.5% (low 62.5%), median 243 ft.
- **As a tie-breaker:** where the report spot and GPS disagree (1,879), the postmile sides with GPS 568 times, with the
  report 514 times, with both 106 times and with neither 691 times. It doesn't break the tie. The urban agencies with
  the most opposite-side misses (9370, 9340, 9580) filled it on 1 to 7 crashes.
